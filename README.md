Game title:   TIme-Out: "BounceBack"


What it's about: Time Out is a charming 2D platformer about a girl on a journey through the sky to fix one enormous broken clock. Jump across floating platforms, dodge lightning strikes, overcome obstacles, and bring the hands of time back to life. Sometimes, all it takes is one girl to get the world ticking agai

How to play and controls: Play in up, down, left, right button, space is jump. x2 space is double-jump, E-interactive with world/item
